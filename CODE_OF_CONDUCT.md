# Code of Conduct

Keep issues, pull requests and discussions useful to people trying to understand or improve the project.

- Discuss code, methods and evidence with respect. Explain disagreements with concrete observations.
- Welcome questions and corrections. Distinguish measured results from assumptions and future work.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or personally attack another participant.
- Do not publish another person's private information, credentials or location data.
- Do not repeatedly disrupt a technical discussion or pressure someone to share private material.

If a discussion becomes unproductive, ask for a specific change in behavior and return to the technical issue. A maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, close a discussion or restrict participation to protect the project and its contributors. Decisions should be based on the behavior in context.

Report conduct concerns through the relevant GitHub discussion or GitHub's reporting tools. Keep personal information out of public reports. This policy does not establish a private contact address or promise a particular response time.
